# Privacy Policy (Bari3)

[日本語](privacy)

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how the app "Bari3" (the "App") handles your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information stored on your device
- Your best score and play count for each game
- How many times you have launched the App and how many days since your first launch
- Your sound on/off setting
- Your language setting (Japanese / English)

This information is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers.

### Information sent to an external service

**PostHog (usage analytics)**  
To improve the App, we use PostHog to collect information about how the App is used. **Data is sent only if you choose "Sure, I'll share" when you first open the App.** If you choose "No thanks", nothing is sent. You can play every game either way. The information collected is:
- Basic app activity, such as opening the App and moving it to the background
- How games are played (which game you started, finished, or quit; your score; how long you played; whether it was a new best; how the game ended; how many times you have played that game; how smoothly the screen was drawn)
- Turning sound on or off
- The language you are using (Japanese / English)
- How many times you have launched the App and how many days since your first launch
- Details of app errors
- Information that PostHog adds automatically, such as device type, OS version, and app version
- An anonymous identifier issued by PostHog

We do not send information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or location.  
Because of how the internet works, PostHog's servers receive information such as your IP address. PostHog may use your IP address to estimate an approximate location (such as country or region). Data is processed on PostHog's servers in the United States.  
Reference: <https://posthog.com/privacy>

The App has no ads and no in-app purchases, and does not collect any information related to them.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To improve the App and analyze how it is used (PostHog)
- To find and fix bugs

## 3. Retention, Deletion, and Opting Out
- Data on your device (scores and settings) is deleted when you delete the App.
- For data held by the external service (PostHog):
  - **Retention**: Determined by PostHog's plan. Currently, data is kept for one year after it is sent (this may change if the plan changes).
  - **Deletion**: Data is linked only to an anonymous identifier and cannot identify you by name or email address. For this reason, we usually cannot single out and delete one person's data. If you would like your data deleted, please contact us at the address below. Because we cannot see your device's identifier, what we can do is limited.
  - **Opting out (withdrawing consent)**: You can switch "Share play data" to OFF at any time from the game list (and switch it back ON). Once it is OFF, nothing more is sent. To request deletion of data already sent, please contact us at the address below.
- Data held by PostHog is also subject to PostHog's own privacy policy.

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties
Except for the PostHog service described above, we do not share your information with third parties (unless required by law).
