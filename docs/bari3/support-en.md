## FAQ

**There is no sound**
- Press "Sound:ON/OFF" at the bottom left of the screen and make sure it says "Sound:ON"
- Also check your iPhone's silent switch and volume

**My best scores disappeared**
- Scores are saved on your device. Deleting the App also deletes your scores

**I want to switch between English and Japanese**
- On the game list, select the "言語 / Language" row and press OK

**How do I play?**
- Press OK (the center button) to start a game. While playing, any key works for jumping and other actions
- Press the right soft key to pause

**Is the game in English?**
- Yes. Bari3 recreates the feel of Japanese flip phones (keitai) from the 2000s. The game text is available in English and Japanese, while some signs and the keypad keep their Japanese look on purpose
