## FAQ

**How do I open Parent Settings?**
- Press and hold the ⚙️ icon at the top right of the screen for 3 seconds

**How do I add or edit tasks?**
- You can add and edit tasks in Parent Settings

**What is the Family Pack?**
- A one-time purchase that lets you add profiles for a second child and more
- Buy it once and keep it forever

**I want to use the Family Pack on another device**
- Use "Restore purchases" in Parent Settings (you need to use the same Apple ID)

**How do I stop sharing usage data?**
- Turn off "Share usage data" in Parent Settings. See the [privacy policy](privacy-en) for details

**Is the App in English?**
- Yes. The App follows your iPhone's language: Japanese if your iPhone is set to Japanese, English otherwise. If your iPhone has more than one preferred language, you can also choose the App's language in the iPhone Settings app (Settings > Apps > Oshitaku Quest > Language)

**Can I move my data to another device?**
- Not at this time
