# Privacy Policy (Oshitaku Quest)

[日本語](privacy)

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the app "Oshitaku Quest" (the "App") handles information. Because children use some screens of the App, we describe clearly, for parents, what we collect and why.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information stored on your device
- Your child's profile, such as name, icon, and theme color
- Morning routine settings (task names, emoji, order, on/off) and the departure time
- Task progress and weekly stamps
- Settings such as sound effects and order lock, and your answer about sharing usage data

This information stays on your device and is not sent to our servers or any external service. **We never send your child's name, the content of tasks, or what your child taps to any external service.**

### Information sent to external services

**PostHog (usage analytics) — only if a parent chooses "Yes, share"**  
To improve the App, we use PostHog to collect usage data. **Data is sent only if a parent chooses "Yes, share" on the screen shown when the App is first opened.** If you choose "No thanks", or until you choose, nothing is sent. The App works fully either way. The information sent is:
- App launches, moving the App to the background, installs, and updates
- Actions taken by parents (opening Parent Settings, setting a departure time, adding a child's profile, opening the purchase screen, completing a purchase)
- Details of app errors
- The language the App is displayed in (Japanese / English)
- Information that PostHog adds automatically, such as device type, OS version, and app version
- An anonymous identifier issued by PostHog

Because of how the internet works, PostHog's servers receive information such as your IP address. PostHog may use your IP address to estimate an approximate location (such as country or region). Data is processed on PostHog's servers in the United States.  
Reference: <https://posthog.com/privacy>

**RevenueCat (in-app purchase management)**  
We use RevenueCat to process and restore purchases of the Family Pack (a one-time purchase). RevenueCat handles an anonymous identifier and purchase history to manage purchases. Because this is required for purchases to work, it is sent regardless of your "Yes, share / No thanks" choice. We do not send names or email addresses.  
Reference: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/>

**Apple (in-app purchases)**  
Payments for the Family Pack are handled through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple manages your payment information; we do not receive it.  
Reference: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

The App has no ads. We do not collect information for advertising and do not do behavioral targeting.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To find and fix bugs, and to analyze usage to improve the App (PostHog)
- To manage purchases and restores of the Family Pack (RevenueCat)

We do not use the information for any other purpose.

## 3. Retention, Deletion, and Opting Out
- Data on your device (profiles, tasks, and settings) is deleted when you delete the App.
- Data sent to PostHog:
  - **Retention**: Determined by PostHog's plan. Currently, data is kept for at least one year after it is sent, and may be deleted after that (this may change if the plan changes).
  - **Opting out (withdrawing consent)**: You can turn "Share usage data" OFF at any time in Parent Settings (press and hold the icon at the top right for 3 seconds), and turn it back ON. Once it is OFF, nothing more is sent, and the anonymous identifier stored on the device is cleared.
  - **Deletion**: Data is linked only to an anonymous identifier and cannot identify anyone by name or email address. For this reason, we usually cannot single out and delete one person's data. If you would like your data deleted, please contact us at the address below. Because we cannot see your device's identifier, what we can do is limited.
- Data held by external services is also subject to each service's own privacy policy.

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties
Except for the external services described above (PostHog, RevenueCat, and Apple), we do not share your information with third parties (unless required by law).

## 5. For Parents
- The App is set up by a parent, and children use it by tapping their tasks. Please make the choice about sharing usage data as a parent.
- To ask about the information we receive, to stop sharing, or to request deletion, please contact us at the address below.
